# Order Repair Changelog

An order-repair pass on the chapters whose "Order Builds to Arrival" score fell after the cut (30 chapters with a drop of 30 points or more), plus all 11 Part Five chapters. The editors were told to fix only what the gap audit and the reviewers' order notes flagged: rewire first, move second, restore reworked third, add a landing last. They could also say "no change needed". Then the touched chapters were rescored by the same ten personas, alongside 10 untouched control chapters.

## Read this first

1. **Result.** Of 37 touched chapters, 22 rose on the Order criterion, 5 held and 10 fell; median score moved +0.16 on average. The 10 untouched controls moved -0.20 (Order criterion -10 pts) with identical text, which is the scoring noise between rounds. **Net of that noise: median +0.36, Order +28 points.** **Read that as direction, not size:** the controls started much stronger (mean median 3.05 against 2.32 for the touched chapters), and chapters chosen for a low score tend to rise on any rescoring while strong ones drift down, so the net figures flatter the repair. Chapters scoring below 3 among the touched: 26 before the repair, 20 after.
2. **Part Five** (the Standing Thirds): median 2 after the cut → 3 after the order repair → **3** after 13 deleted figures were restored (mean 2.2 → 2.7 → 3.1). See "Part Five figures restored".
3. **What was changed.** 74 paragraphs rewired, 15 short paragraphs added (reworked bridges, restores and landings), 2 paragraphs moved. 2 chapters needed nothing. 109 flagged items were skipped as not worth fixing. Net length 71,795 → 73,161 in-scope words (+1366).
4. **Moves are rare.** The editors judged most order problems to be missing definitions and cold openings (fixed by rewiring), not misplaced paragraphs. One of three requested moves was rejected because it would cross a heading or table boundary (the applier only reorders within a stretch of consecutive paragraphs).
5. **Some repairs also trimmed.** Where the reviewers' order notes were about a duplicated list or stacked strands (for example the move list in the Part Two opener), the editor rewrote the paragraph shorter. Those edits show a negative word delta below.
6. `reconcile` is clean and no anchor phrase was lost. Headings are unchanged.
