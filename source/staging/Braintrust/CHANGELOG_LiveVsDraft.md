# Live Manuscript vs. Draft v8: Summary Changelog

**Compared:** `source/Fractalism_Complete.md` on `main` (commit `adb763a`, the manuscript the site's 84 chapter pages and 53 Standing Thirds were redrawn from) against `source/staging/Braintrust/Fractalism_Draft_v8_DyadStructure.md`. I cannot see the deployed site, so "live" here means that manuscript. Nothing under `src/` or in the source manuscript has been changed.

## The short version

v8 is a substantially different text from the live manuscript, not an edited copy. Same skeleton, 12% less body text, most of the wording new.

| | Live | v8 |
|---|---|---|
| Whole file, words | 93,740 | 85,650 (-8.6%) |
| Body text (Front Matter to Appendix A), words | 82,643 | 72,082 (-12.8%) |
| Paragraphs in that body | 1,090 | 810 |
| Chapters / Part order / chapter titles | 91 | 91, same titles, same order |
| Headings | 120 | 126 (adds Appendix E and its five ring subheads) |

**How much of the live wording survives.** Two lenses, because they answer different questions:
- **Exact wording** (runs of six identical words): **23%** of the live manuscript's wording survives in v8, and about 74% of v8's wording does not appear in the live text. This is harsh on light edits, since changing one word breaks several six-word runs.
- **Paragraphs:** of 1,090 live paragraphs, **52 are word-for-word identical (5%)** and 331 are recognizably the same paragraph reworded. The other 707 are gone or rewritten beyond recognition. 427 paragraphs in v8 have no close match in the live text (new, or rewritten beyond recognition). Those last two groups overlap, since a heavily rewritten paragraph counts in both.
- **No chapter is untouched.** The only unchanged block is the Appendix A opener line.

## How it got from there to here

Each step has its own changelog in this folder.

1. **Paragraph review (Self scale).** Ten personas scored all 1,090 paragraphs. 638 were revised, 40 cut, 320 kept as written, over up to four rounds. 24 Derivation anchors the revisions broke were restored by hand. (`CHANGELOG.md`)
2. **Seams, chapters, Parts, whole book (Dyad to World).** 397 seam rewrites (+4,970 words), 435 chapter edits and 46 new bridging paragraphs, 13 Part-level edits and 3 paragraphs. (`CHANGELOG_Scales.md`, `Rubrics.md`)
3. **Fixes from the retrospective.** Part-level fixes (19 edits, 12 new paragraphs), 38 chapters rebuilt (369 edits, 17 new paragraphs), 56 seams reworked. (`CHANGELOG_Fixes.md`, `Retrospective.md`)
4. **Extreme cut.** 340 paragraphs removed (31,176 words), 211 edited to carry a folded sentence. A ten-persona gap audit found what the cut broke; 66 reworked edits and 2 paragraphs were added back. (`CHANGELOG_Cut.md`, `Cut_Ledger.md` holds every removed paragraph.)
5. **Order repair.** 74 edits, 15 new paragraphs and 2 moves across 37 chapters. 13 Standing Thirds rows the cut had dropped were restored. (`CHANGELOG_OrderRepair.md`)
6. **Table shelf.** New Appendix E holds all 52 figure rows; each ring chapter keeps 3 to 5 teaching rows plus a find-it list. (`CHANGELOG_TableDiet.md`)
7. **Dyad-Kin and Deck.** Forward and backward folded together, stable-versus-stalled cut to a sentence, Deck reduced to its ring questions. (`CHANGELOG_DyadDeck.md`, `CHANGELOG_DyadDeckStructural.md`)
