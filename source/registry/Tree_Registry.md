# The Tree of Thirds — canonical registry

One entry per figure. Rules: Staging Z (supersedes Y1). Quotes behind every mark: `Tree_Evidence.json`. Figures merged, renamed or unseated: `Tree_Unseated.json` and the last section below.

**Poles (2026-10-01).** Tending = the loop strengthening; Fraying = the loop weakening; neither is good or bad (see `harness/poles/GUIDE.md`). Pole evidence: `Tree_PoleEvidence.json`; the earlier sentiment-based poles are kept under `old_poles` in the JSON.

**Marks.** Three elements per figure — composition (C), Tending (T), Fraying (F) — each counting independent arrivals (0 ○, 1 ★, 2+ ★★). The figure's mark is its weakest element. Every composition is written *A + B (via L)*, where L is the leg the two parts share: a Self for a Skein, a Dyad for a Culture.
