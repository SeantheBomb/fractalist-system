**A fusion of [The Easier Door](/thirds/the-easier-door/) and [The Spark](/thirds/the-spark/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each stricter line is met faster by a new way just past it, and each new way brings a stricter line sooner..

**Fraying.** New ways around the line come slower and rarer, and the line gets tightened less often..

**Its Turning.** Look. What it builds: purpose outranks letter.
