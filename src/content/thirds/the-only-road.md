**A fusion of [The Unbridged Gap](/thirds/the-unbridged-gap/) and [Filling the Gap](/thirds/filling-the-gap/)**, sharing a person. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** The single bridge by which two separate worlds reach each other at all.

**Fraying.** One person through whom everything must pass, whose absence severs rather than inconveniences.

**Its Turning.** **The Handover** — whoever everything routes through hands one thing over completely, in advance, and does not take it back. What it builds: indispensability gets converted back into redundancy.

Catalogued in [The Skein-Kin](/node/skein-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
