**A fusion of [The Rising Bar](/thirds/the-rising-bar/) and [Escalation](/thirds/escalation/)**, sharing a person. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each win pays out more to spend on the next round, so the leader pulls ahead faster and the gap widens quicker..

**Fraying.** Each win pays out less toward the next round, so the lead grows slower and the trailing side can close in..

**Its Turning.** Thin. What it builds: early advantage stops calcifying.

Catalogued in [The Skein-Kin](/node/skein-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
