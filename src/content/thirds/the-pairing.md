**A fusion of [The Spark](/thirds/the-spark/) and [The Tide](/thirds/the-tide/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** One drives, one steadies — a real division of labor.

**Fraying.** One always pushing, one always braking, each resenting the role.

**Its Turning.** **Trading Places** — swap the driver and the brake for a day. What it builds: each learns the cost of the other's role.

Catalogued in [The Dyad-Kin](/node/dyad-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
