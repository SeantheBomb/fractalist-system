**A fusion of [The Spark](/thirds/the-spark/) and [The Tide](/thirds/the-tide/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** The steady side answers each push harder and sooner, so the pair holds closer to one level..

**Fraying.** The steady side answers each push more loosely, so the push runs further before it is pulled back, or keeps going..

**Its Turning.** Look. What it builds: each learns the cost of the other's role.
