**A fusion of [The Salve](/thirds/the-salve/) and [The Spark](/thirds/the-spark/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each quick answer leaves the asker less able to work things out, so the asks come faster and lean harder..

**Fraying.** Answers come slower or less often, so the asker works more out alone and asks less each time..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: capacity stays with its owner.

Catalogued in [The Dyad-Kin](/node/dyad-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
