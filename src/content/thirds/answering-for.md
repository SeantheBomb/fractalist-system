**A fusion of [The Salve](/thirds/the-salve/) and [The Spark](/thirds/the-spark/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Expertise given generously where it is genuinely needed.

**Fraying.** Expertise that prevents them developing their own.

**Its Turning.** **The Held Tongue** — a conversation where you may only ask questions. What it builds: capacity stays with its owner.
