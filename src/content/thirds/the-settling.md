**A fusion of [The Sliding Floor](/thirds/the-sliding-floor/) and [The Standoff](/thirds/the-standoff/)**, sharing a person. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each small slip in what the group expects makes the next slip easier, and the level sinks faster..

**Fraying.** Each slip pulls the group's level down less, and expectations sink slower before they hold..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: the group remembers what its ceiling was.

Catalogued in [The Skein-Kin](/node/skein-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
