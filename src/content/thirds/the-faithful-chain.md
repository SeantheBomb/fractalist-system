**A fusion of [Answering For](/thirds/answering-for/) and [Watering Another](/thirds/watering-another/)**, sharing a person. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each learner copies the teacher more closely and passes on more of the steps, even the ones no one can explain..

**Fraying.** Each learner copies less closely and passes fewer of the steps on, so the set changes or thins along the chain..

**Its Turning.** Tend. What it builds: skill compounds with its lineage intact.

Catalogued in [The Skein-Kin](/node/skein-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
