**A fusion of [Gaming the Terms](/thirds/gaming-the-terms/) and [The Rising Bar](/thirds/the-rising-bar/)**, sharing a person. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** What the rules make shows up more clearly to those who set them, and each rule change follows it more closely..

**Fraying.** What the rules produce reaches the rule makers less clearly, and the rules drift further from what they cause..

**Its Turning.** Look. What it builds: the group feels the return path, not just the emergence.
