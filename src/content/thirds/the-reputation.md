**A fusion of [Typecasting](/thirds/typecasting/) and [Watering Another](/thirds/watering-another/)**, sharing a person. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** The group leans on a person's role more, and the role grips them tighter each time they fill it..

**Fraying.** The group leans on the role less, and the person drifts in and out of it more freely..

**Its Turning.** Thin. What it builds: group-authored roles can be returned.

Catalogued in [The Skein-Kin](/node/skein-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
