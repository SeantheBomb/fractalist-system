**A fusion of [The Naming Contest](/thirds/the-naming-contest/) and [The Tested Price](/thirds/the-tested-price/)**, sharing a person. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** More short words get made and shared, and newcomers must learn them faster to join in, or stay out..

**Fraying.** The group's short words fade from use, so talk grows plainer and newcomers join with less to learn..

**Its Turning.** Thin. What it builds: fluency stops functioning as a wall.

Catalogued in [The Skein-Kin](/node/skein-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
