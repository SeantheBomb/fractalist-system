**The Local Rule.** Storing an episode by its peak and its close. This is a Self-Kin figure: one person, repeating one thing. Everything further out in the catalogue is built by fusing figures like this one together.

**Tending.** Each time is kept more by its high point and its ending, and the rest fades from memory faster..

**Fraying.** The high point and ending hold less sway, so more of the whole time stays in memory as it was lived..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: the remembered life reconnects to the lived one.
