**A fusion of [Gaming the Terms](/thirds/gaming-the-terms/) and [Escalation](/thirds/escalation/)**, sharing a person. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each side's taking or care lands on the other harder, so the other takes or cares more, faster..

**Fraying.** Each side's taking or care lands on the other less, so the other's answer grows weaker and slower..

**Its Turning.** Plant. What it builds: shared things acquire a keeper.

Catalogued in [The Skein-Kin](/node/skein-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
