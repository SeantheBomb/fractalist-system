**[The Mirror](/thirds/the-mirror/), fused with itself** — the same pattern running on both sides, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each reading draws the part out more surely, and each part played makes the next reading firmer..

**Fraying.** The reading draws the part out less, and each part played proves the view more weakly..

**Its Turning.** Thin. What it builds: two positions survive contact.
