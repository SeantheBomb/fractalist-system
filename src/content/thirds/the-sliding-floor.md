**A fusion of [The Mark](/thirds/the-mark/) and [The Tide](/thirds/the-tide/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** The bar tracks each last result more closely, so effort and results climb or sink faster as one..

**Fraying.** The bar follows the last result less, so effort and results move more slowly and steady out..

**Its Turning.** Look. What it builds: drift becomes visible while it's still small.

Catalogued in [The Dyad-Kin](/node/dyad-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
