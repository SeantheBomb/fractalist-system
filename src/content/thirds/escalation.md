**[The Spark](/thirds/the-spark/), fused with itself** — the same pattern running on both sides, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each side aims higher faster, and every step up pushes the other's next aim further still..

**Fraying.** Each side aims a little less above the other, and the climb slows until the steps level off..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: escalation gets a reliable off-ramp.

Catalogued in [The Dyad-Kin](/node/dyad-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
