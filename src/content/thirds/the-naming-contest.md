**[The Namer](/thirds/the-namer/), fused with itself** — the same pattern running on both sides, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each name is met faster with the other side's name, so the naming back and forth grows quicker and more insistent..

**Fraying.** Each name is met more slowly or not at all, so the naming back and forth quiets and the words settle loosely..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: vocabulary becomes shared rather than contested.

Catalogued in [The Dyad-Kin](/node/dyad-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
