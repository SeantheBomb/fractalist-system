**The Local Rule.** Taking form from who is present. This is a Self-Kin figure: one person, repeating one thing. Everything further out in the catalogue is built by fusing figures like this one together.

**Tending.** The self takes its shape more fully and faster from whoever is present, matching each new person more closely..

**Fraying.** The self takes less of its shape from who is present, holding more of the same form across different people..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: you find what's left when nobody's watching.
