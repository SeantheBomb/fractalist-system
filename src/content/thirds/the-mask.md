**The Local Rule.** Maintaining a self-story against evidence. This is a Self-Kin figure: one person, repeating one thing. Everything further out in the catalogue is built by fusing figures like this one together.

**Tending.** The story about who one is holds tighter against each new fact, and each fact pushed aside makes it surer..

**Fraying.** The story about who one is loosens as facts pile up, and each crack lets more of what is seen slip in..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: self-stories stay testable.
