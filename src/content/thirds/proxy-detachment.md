**A fusion of [Second-Order Design](/thirds/second-order-design/) and [The Shared Pot](/thirds/the-shared-pot/)**, compounded past any one life. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Effort aims harder at where the number moves, so the number climbs faster and is called a win more surely..

**Fraying.** Effort aims less at the number, so it climbs slower and is called a win less often..

**Its Turning.** Thin. What it builds: the measure stops outranking what it measured.

Catalogued in [The Culture-Kin](/node/culture-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
