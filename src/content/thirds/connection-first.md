**A fusion of [The Namer](/thirds/the-namer/) and [The Tide](/thirds/the-tide/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Warmth comes first sooner each time, and each warm start makes what follows land with less resistance..

**Fraying.** Warmth comes first less often, and what follows meets more resistance and lands more slowly..

**Its Turning.** Tend. What it builds: content lands because connection preceded it.
