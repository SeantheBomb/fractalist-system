**A fusion of [The Mask](/thirds/the-mask/) and [The Mirror](/thirds/the-mirror/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** The more one person knows, the harder it gets to picture a mind without that knowing, and the gap widens..

**Fraying.** Knowing more blocks the view of other minds less, and the gap between them slowly narrows..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: expertise stays transmissible.

Catalogued in [The Dyad-Kin](/node/dyad-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
