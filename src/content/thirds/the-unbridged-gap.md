**A fusion of [The Mask](/thirds/the-mask/) and [The Mirror](/thirds/the-mirror/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Fluency that comes from real depth.

**Fraying.** Inability to model a mind that lacks your context.

**Its Turning.** **The Teaching** — teach each other something from your own domain, to a beginner standard. What it builds: expertise stays transmissible.
