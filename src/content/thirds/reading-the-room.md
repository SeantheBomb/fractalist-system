**A fusion of [The Nearest Light](/thirds/the-nearest-light/) and [The Mirror](/thirds/the-mirror/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Each side catches the other's tension faster and closer, so the two moods hold more tightly matched..

**Fraying.** Each side catches less of the other's tension, so the two moods drift further apart before they line up again..

**Its Turning.** Look. What it builds: projection separates from perception.

Catalogued in [The Dyad-Kin](/node/dyad-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
