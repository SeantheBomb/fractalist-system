**A fusion of [First-Arriver Lock-In](/thirds/first-arriver-lock-in/) and [Second-Order Design](/thirds/second-order-design/)**, compounded past any one life. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** The people in the place hold each other to the easiest way more tightly, so the setup stays fixed more firmly..

**Fraying.** The people hold each other to the easiest way more loosely, so other ways of acting spread and the setup shifts..

**Its Turning.** Look. What it builds: what has held fixed too long to notice becomes visible once a year.

Catalogued in [The Culture-Kin](/node/culture-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
