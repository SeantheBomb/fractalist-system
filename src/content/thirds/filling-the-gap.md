**A fusion of [The Mask](/thirds/the-mask/) and [The Nearest Light](/thirds/the-nearest-light/)**, meeting in two people. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** The early story holds tighter, and more of what the other does gets bent back to fit it..

**Fraying.** The early story loosens, and what the other does bends less to fit, so the picture drifts..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: assumptions get tested before they harden.

Catalogued in [The Dyad-Kin](/node/dyad-kin/), alongside every other figure at this scale.
