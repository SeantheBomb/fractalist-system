**A fusion of [The Faithful Chain](/thirds/the-faithful-chain/) and [The Reputation](/thirds/the-reputation/)**, compounded past any one life. A figure's tier is what it's made of, not a rank it was given.

**Tending.** Handed-down ways are followed more surely with each passing, and the reasons behind them matter less to anyone..

**Fraying.** Handed-down ways are followed less surely, and people ask more often why they were ever done..

**Its Turning.** Fray. What it builds: inheritance keeps its reasons attached.
