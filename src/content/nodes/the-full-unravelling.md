Reserved for large, rare questions, and built differently from every other spread here — because this is the one that uses the thing that makes this deck unlike any other deck: the figures are *composed of each other*, and the composition can be walked.

A Four-Point <a class="concept-link" href="/concept/weaving/">Weaving</a> draws a card per ring and reads them side by side. This one draws twice and derives the rest.

**Draw two Self-Kin.** Not one per ring — two from the innermost ring only, returning the first before drawing the second, since both sides can be running the same pattern. One is the pattern you are running. The other is the pattern running at you, whether that is another person's or simply the situation's.

**Then stop drawing, and read upward.**

Those two Self-Kin may fuse into a Dyad-Kin, though most pairs do not: where the catalogue lists nothing for yours, the two patterns are running side by side and making nothing together, and that is the reading. Where they do fuse, the catalogue gives the pair one figure. Look it up rather than drawing it, and check that it is actually present, as you will at every step above it. That figure is what the two patterns produce together, and it is derived rather than drawn, which means chance has nothing further to say about it.

That Dyad-Kin, paired with another that shares a Self-Kin with it, names a Skein-Kin — and here you choose, from the small set the catalogue allows, because you are the one who knows which of those pairings is actually present in your life. The choosing is the reading. Then the same move once more, upward, to a Culture-Kin if the question reaches that far, and once again to a World-Kin only if the question is genuinely about the world. Most don't.

**What this produces that a Four-Point cannot:** a Four-Point shows you four angles on one situation, side by side, and leaves you to relate them. The Full Unravelling shows you one situation at up to five depths, with each depth *accounting for* the one below it. You are not collecting views. You are watching a small thing at the self scale turn into a large thing you had been treating as simply given, and seeing the steps.

It is slow on purpose, and the slowness is not ceremony — most of the time is spent at the derivation steps, deciding honestly which pairing is real. A Full Unravelling rushed through in ten minutes has skipped the only part of it that was doing any work.

**The limit, stated plainly.** The chain terminates before <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/infinite-boundary/">the Infinite Boundary</a>, which is never drawn and never derived. There is no move upward past the World-Kin, which is exactly what the Boundary means. If your reading seems to reach past it, you have left the practice and started telling a story about the universe.
