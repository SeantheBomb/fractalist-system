These twelve are generated directly, by one person, repeating one thing. They fuse into everything that follows.

| Third | The <a class="concept-link" href="/concept/local-rule/">Local Rule</a> | Tending | Fraying |
|---|---|---|---|
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-spark/">The Spark</a>** | An output becomes the next input | Each result feeds the next turn more, so whatever is growing grows faster and faster. | Each result feeds the next turn less, so the growth slows and fades. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-tide/">The Tide</a>** | Returning toward a setpoint | Each time it is pushed away, the pull back to its usual level comes faster and holds tighter. | The pull back loosens, so each push carries it further off before it returns. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-mark/">The Mark</a>** | Judging against a reference point | Each judgment leans harder on the old mark it is measured against, and the mark holds firmer the more it is used. | Judgments lean less on the old mark, and it fades until it barely shapes what gets weighed. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-salve/">The Salve</a>** | Reaching for what relieves the symptom | Each easing makes the next reach for relief come sooner and surer, so relief is sought more often and faster. | The reach for relief comes slower and less often, and the symptom is left to sit longer before anything is done. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-nearest-light/">The Nearest Light</a>** | Judging by what arrives first | Whatever comes to mind first weighs more and more, and each quick judgment makes the next first thought arrive faster. | What comes to mind first weighs less, and judgments wait longer for what arrives later. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-keepsake/">The Keepsake</a>** | Storing an episode by its peak and its close | Each time is kept more by its high point and its ending, and the rest fades from memory faster. | The high point and ending hold less sway, so more of the whole time stays in memory as it was lived. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-easier-door/">The Easier Door</a>** | Answering a simpler question than the one asked | A hard question gets swapped for an easier one more quickly and more often, and the easy answer feels surer. | The swap happens less often, so more hard questions are met as asked, slowly and with less ease. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-mask/">The Mask</a>** | Maintaining a self-story against evidence | The story about who one is holds tighter against each new fact, and each fact pushed aside makes it surer. | The story about who one is loosens as facts pile up, and each crack lets more of what is seen slip in. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-mirror/">The Mirror</a>** | Taking form from who is present | The self takes its shape more fully and faster from whoever is present, matching each new person more closely. | The self takes less of its shape from who is present, holding more of the same form across different people. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-namer/">The Namer</a>** | Speaking as though words build what follows | Words spoken about what comes next build it faster and surer, each naming shaping more of what follows. | Spoken words shape less of what follows, and what gets named drifts apart from what gets built. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-riverbed/">The Riverbed</a>** | Shaping conditions so one action is easiest | The setup makes one action easier and easier, so it happens more often with less thought. | The setup steers less, so the action takes more effort and happens less often without thought. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-gardener/">The Gardener</a>** | Attending repeatedly to one thing among many | Attention goes back to the same thing more often, and that thing grows larger and deeper with each visit. | Attention drifts to other things, and the one it used to visit grows slower and starts to fade. |

**One thing worth saying about the Tide specifically, because it corrects the most common misreading of balance.** A balancing loop rarely produces stillness. With enough delay in it, it produces a wave — a continuous teeter around its setpoint, corrected constantly, whose average looks like rest and whose actual state never is. Note too that the Tide's Fraying pole is not a pull that comes back too soon — a pull that hauls back early is the Tide holding harder, which is Tending. Fraying is the correction loosening; at its end the loop *stops altogether*, which is no longer a pole but a pattern that has quit running, and the thing careens, however long it held before. Balance is a rate of work you sustain, never a state you reach.

*Four practices that behave like Self-Kin but are not Thirds — calibrating difficulty to skill, absorbed effort, shaping demands to your current state, and conditioning a state by cue — live in Part Four's chapter on flow instead. They are skillful engagement with loops, not loops in their own right, and they fuse into nothing.*
