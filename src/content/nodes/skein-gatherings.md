These are not dated and they are not monthly. A Skein already gathers — a standing dinner, a practice night, a group that happens to be in one room — and these are what you reach for once you are there and something in the room is asking for it.

Which one you reach for is closer to reading a Dyad Turning than to consulting a calendar, with one difference: a Dyad Turning is triggered by a circumstance you can usually name out loud, and a Gathering is usually sensed before it is named. Someone notices the group has stopped saying hard things, or that everything routes through one person, or that the standard slid. That noticing is the trigger. Run the matching Gathering at the next occasion you are already having, and don't schedule a new occasion to accommodate it.

|---|---|---|---|
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/held-silence/">Held Silence</a> | Thin | **The Loud Table** — the table is opened for one true unspoken thing, and the silence that follows if nobody takes it is left unfilled | Saying the hard thing stops being unthinkable |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/transmission-drift/">Transmission Drift</a> | Look | **Round the Circle** — play telephone with a real story about the group | Drift becomes funny and visible |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-faithful-chain/">The Faithful Chain</a> | Tend | **The Handing-On** — teach one craft; credit whoever taught you, out loud | Skill compounds with its lineage intact |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/first-arriver-lock-in/">First-Arriver Lock-In</a> | Thin | **The Newcomer's Turn** — the newest member decides what the oldest usually decides | Early advantage stops calcifying |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-shared-pot/">The Shared Pot</a> | Plant | **The Common Purse** — a resource everyone adds to and anyone may draw from, tracked openly | Shared things acquire a keeper |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-reputation/">The Reputation</a> | Thin | **The Recasting** — each names a role in the group they're tired of holding | Group-authored roles can be returned |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-settling/">The Settling</a> | Thin | **The High Bar** — attempt one genuinely difficult thing together | The group remembers what its ceiling was |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-hall-of-mirrors/">The Hall of Mirrors</a> | Look | **The Outsider** — invite someone outside and let them describe what they see | An independent position enters the room |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/second-order-design/">Second-Order Design</a> | Look | **The Rule Game** — invent a game with three rules, play it, then change one rule in answer to what actually happened | The group feels the return path, not just the emergence |

| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-only-road/">The Only Road</a> | Thin | **The Handover** — whoever everything routes through hands one thing over completely, in advance, and does not take it back | Indispensability gets converted back into redundancy |
