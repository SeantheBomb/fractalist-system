| Weave | The Turning | What it builds |
|---|---|---|
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/held-silence/">Held Silence</a> | **The Loud Table** — someone must say one true unspoken thing before dessert | Silence stops being the group's default |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/transmission-drift/">Transmission Drift</a> | **Round the Circle** — play telephone with a real story about the group | Drift becomes funny and visible |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-faithful-chain/">The Faithful Chain</a> | **The Handing-On** — teach one craft; credit whoever taught you, out loud | Skill compounds with its lineage intact |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/first-arriver-lock-in/">First-Arriver Lock-In</a> | **The Newcomer's Turn** — the newest member decides what the oldest usually decides | Early advantage stops calcifying |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-shared-pot/">The Shared Pot</a> | **The Common Purse** — a resource everyone adds to and anyone may draw from, tracked openly | Shared things acquire a keeper |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-reputation/">The Reputation</a> | **The Recasting** — each names a role in the group they're tired of holding | Group-authored roles can be returned |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-sagging-floor/">The Sagging Floor</a> | **The High Bar** — attempt one genuinely difficult thing together | The group remembers what its ceiling was |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-hall-of-mirrors/">The Hall of Mirrors</a> | **The Outsider** — invite someone outside and let them describe what they see | An independent position enters the room |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/second-order-design/">Second-Order Design</a> | **The Rule Game** — invent a game with three rules, play it, watch what emerges | The group feels emergence rather than being told about it |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-inner-circle/">The Inner Circle</a> | **The Plain Speech** — a gathering where no in-jokes or shorthand are allowed | Fluency stops functioning as a wall |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-only-road/">The Only Road</a> | **The Handover** — whoever everything routes through spends one gathering entirely absent, on purpose, with the group carrying on | Indispensability gets converted back into redundancy |
