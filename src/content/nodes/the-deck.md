The full working deck is the complete drawable roster from Part Five — fifty-two figures across five drawable tiers, each carrying two readings, Tending and Fraying, described fully in the next chapter — plus <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/infinite-boundary/">the Infinite Boundary</a>, which is never drawn at all. For scale: a tarot deck runs seventy-eight cards and a poker deck fifty-two, so this is the same size as the second and smaller than the first. The size is not the point; the coverage is. A roster small enough to find your way around, and wide enough that a pattern has somewhere to be found when you finally meet it, is doing something a short list cannot.

What follows is a compressed reference, not a replacement for Part Five's catalogue — read there for the mechanism and the fusion lineage; use this when you're actually weaving and need the meaning at a glance.

**The Self-Kin** *(innermost ring)*

| Third | Tending | Fraying |
|---|---|---|
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-spark/">The Spark</a> | It's catching faster. Each gain feeds the next, and whatever you started is picking up speed. | It's slowing. Each gain feeds the next a little less, and the push you had is fading. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-tide/">The Tide</a> | You're snapping back to your usual level faster each time something pulls you off it. | You're drifting further from your usual level each time, and coming back slower. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-mark/">The Mark</a> | You're weighing things against an old mark more, and it holds firmer each time you use it. | The old mark you weigh things against is fading, and it shapes your choices less. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-salve/">The Salve</a> | You're reaching for relief sooner and more often, each easing making the next reach quicker. | You're reaching for relief less often, and letting the ache sit longer before you act. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-nearest-light/">The Nearest Light</a> | Your first thought is weighing more, and each quick call makes the next one come faster. | Your first thought weighs less now. You're waiting longer for what shows up later. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-keepsake/">The Keepsake</a> | You're keeping each time more by its peak and its ending, and the rest is fading faster. | The peak and the ending hold less sway, and more of the whole time stays with you. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-easier-door/">The Easier Door</a> | You're swapping hard questions for easier ones more often, and the easy answers feel surer. | You're swapping less often. More hard questions get met as asked, and more slowly. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-mask/">The Mask</a> | Your story about yourself is holding tighter, and each fact you set aside makes it surer. | Your story about yourself is loosening, and more of what you see is slipping in. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-mirror/">The Mirror</a> | You're taking your shape from whoever is near, faster and more fully with each new person. | You're taking less of your shape from the room, and staying more the same across people. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-namer/">The Namer</a> | What you say about what's next is building it faster, each word shaping more of what follows. | Your words shape less of what follows, and what you name drifts from what gets built. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-riverbed/">The Riverbed</a> | Your setup makes one action easier each time, so it happens more, with less thought. | Your setup steers you less, and that action takes more effort and comes less often. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-gardener/">The Gardener</a> | Your attention keeps coming back to one thing, and it's growing larger and deeper each visit. | Your attention is drifting to other things, and what it used to visit grows slower and fades. |

|---|---|---|

| Third | Tending | Fraying |
|---|---|---|
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-shared-name/">The Shared Name</a> | The name is pulling its people closer, and the closer they get, the more it means. | The name pulls its people together less, and it means less each time it's passed on. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/proxy-detachment/">Proxy Detachment</a> | Effort is aiming harder at moving the number, and the number climbs faster and is called a win more surely. | Effort aims less at the number, and it climbs slower and is called a win less often. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/policy-resistance/">Policy Resistance</a> | Each push to change things meets a harder push back, and the whole holds more firmly. | Each push meets a weaker push back, and the whole drifts further before it returns, or moves. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-long-cycle/">The Long Cycle</a> | Each round of growth feeds the next more, and the rise and the fall are both growing. | Each round feeds the next less, and the swings rise and fall more gently. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-received-world/">The Received World</a> | Handed-down ways are followed more surely, and why they started matters less to anyone. | Handed-down ways are followed less surely, and people ask more often why they began. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-slow-hand/">The Slow Hand</a> | People here hold each other to the easiest way more tightly, and the setup sets firmer. | People hold each other to the easiest way more loosely, and other ways are spreading. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/independent-convergence/">Independent Convergence</a> | More separate seekers are landing on the same answer, and each match makes it surer. | Fewer separate seekers land on the same answer, and each match carries less weight. |

**The World-Kin** *(beyond the four rings, entering a reading only when the question is genuinely about the world)*

| Third | Tending | Fraying |
|---|---|---|
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-standing-account/">The Standing Account</a> | A people is counting itself more closely, and steering by the counts more firmly. | The counts track less closely, and steering by them is growing looser. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/second-nature/">Second Nature</a> | Built ways fit daily life more closely, and the closer they fit, the less anyone notices. | Built ways fit daily life less closely, and people notice more often that someone made them. |
| <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-set-course/">The Set Course</a> | Each thing built on an early choice makes it harder to leave, and the course sets deeper. | Each thing built on the early choice binds it less, and leaving is growing easier. |

**The Cosmos scale — never drawn.** The Infinite Boundary is the one figure in this whole deck that never gets pulled from anything, because there's only one of it and it's already true of every <a class="concept-link" href="/concept/weaving/">Weaving</a> before a single card is turned over. It isn't a ring of its own. It's the edge the other rings already sit inside, present by default, worth naming once at the start of a Weaving rather than drawn as a variable within it — a reminder that whatever the four cards show, the whole reading is still one small, summed part of something no card was ever going to capture on its own.
