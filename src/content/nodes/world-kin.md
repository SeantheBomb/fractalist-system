One tier above Culture, and the last one where you can still push.

These are the only patterns in the catalogue whose parts are Culture-Kin — two of them, sharing a Skein-Kin, exactly as the Skein and Culture tiers are built. Run that rule against the seven Culture figures, leaving aside <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/independent-convergence/">Independent Convergence</a>, whose own fusion is defined by having nothing shared, and it produces three results and no others. Three of the remaining six compose into nothing at all, which is a fact about the catalogue rather than a gap in it: not everything is an ingredient for something larger.

They run on the living world, which is the substrate rather than a figure. The biosphere is a <a class="concept-link" href="/concept/third-thing/">Third Thing</a> — emergent, with weight none of its parts contain — but it is not a Standing Third, because there is no repeated act underneath it. It is the aggregate, not the pattern. What follows are the patterns that run on top of it.

| Third | Culture-Kin | Tending | Fraying |
|---|---|---|---|
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-standing-account/">The Standing Account</a>** | <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/proxy-detachment/">Proxy Detachment</a> + <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-long-cycle/">The Long Cycle</a> | A people count and track themselves more closely, and steer more firmly by what the counts show. | The counts track less closely, and steering by them grows looser and drifts. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/second-nature/">Second Nature</a>** | Proxy Detachment + <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-slow-hand/">The Slow Hand</a> | Built ways fit daily life more closely, and the closer they fit, the less anyone notices they were built. | Built ways fit daily life less closely, and people notice more often that someone made them. |
| **<a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/the-set-course/">The Set Course</a>** | The Long Cycle + The Slow Hand | Each thing built on an early choice makes that choice harder to leave, and the course sets deeper. | Each thing built on the early choice binds it less, and leaving the course grows easier. |

**Why this tier exists, and why it stops here.** Every figure below this can be pushed on by a person, a pair, a group, or a culture. Every figure here can still be pushed on — collectively, by many hands at once, but genuinely. That is the whole distinction. **The tier above this one is where leverage runs out**, and <a class="concept-link" href="/thirds/infinite-boundary/">the Infinite Boundary</a>'s lack of poles is not a special case granted for convenience: it sits where the chain ends, with no scale above it to read a pole from. This tier is what makes that boundary legible instead of arbitrary.

**On reading these honestly, because a world-scale figure is the easiest thing in this catalogue to misuse.** These describe mechanisms, not verdicts, and specifically they do not describe a world with or without this book in it. A pole you can only read by first accepting a framework is not a pole; it is an advertisement wearing one. The test is available and it is worth running whenever one of these feels satisfying: **could someone who rejects everything else in this book read this pole and still agree which way it is currently pointed?** If not, the reading has left the practice.

**And the honest limit on all three.** These are four inference-layers deep, composed from a tier already flagged as provisional. Treat them as the shape of something real rather than as a measurement of it. They are drawable, because leverage reaches them — but the standard four-ring Weaving stays at four rings, since four is what a personal situation occupies. A World-Kin enters a reading when the question is genuinely about the world, and not as a fifth card added for completeness.
